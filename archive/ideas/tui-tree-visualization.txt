# =============================================================================
# NAVIGATION CONTROLS
# =============================================================================

## Navigation Legend
- **Arrow Keys (Up/Down)**: Navigate through menu items
- **Arrow Keys (Left/Right)**: Enter/exit submenus
- **TAB**: Select/deselect packages (toggle selection)
- **ENTER**: Enter submenu or confirm final selection
- **Back Option**: Always available at bottom to go back

## Visual Indicators
- **[SELECTED]**: Package selected with checkmark
- **(ENTER → submenu)**: Indicates submenu can be entered
- **Selection Persistence**: Selected packages remain selected when navigating

## Profile Differences
- **Common User**: 10 modules, limited submodules, no advanced tools
- **Power User**: 12 modules, full submodules, limited advanced tools
- **Expanded (--expanded)**: 12 modules, ALL submodules, complete toolset
